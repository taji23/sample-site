# Surface: Home (and the site-wide visual world)

Scope: homepage `/` (Persuade). The design system built for it is shared by every subpage.
Audience/job: parents on phones deciding whether to book a trial; families checking the next match; sponsors judging credibility.
Constraints: brief-pinned palette and concept (PROJECT_SPEC.md); no real photos yet; all facts fictional and labeled.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A matchday programme made into a website — editorial, numbered, photographic, confident. Refuses the hobby-club template of a centered logo over a row of equal icon cards.

OWN-WORLD: White pitch-clean ground, navy fields that own whole bands (Next Match, Join Us, footer), royal blue as the action color, muted gold reserved for rules, numerals, and the JOIN US CTA. Archivo condensed heavy uppercase for display and numerals with tabular figures; Zen Kaku Gothic New for Japanese. Near-square corners, hairline rules, navy-tinted soft shadows.

STORY: Visitor sees a serious academy, finds their age category, sees real activity (matches, news), and books a trial.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed cinematic photo, headline set huge at bottom-left over a navy gradient, primary trial CTA plus secondary link beside it, Next Match strip overlapping the hero's bottom edge.

FORM: Matchday programme editorial; position 1 on the list; seed: none (brief-pinned direction, no roll). Signature interaction: section numerals and gold rules draw in on scroll; card images ease-scale on hover with expo-out timing.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
