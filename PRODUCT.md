# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JavaScript generated by a zero-dependency Node script (`scripts/build.mjs`) into `dist/`. Every page ships as pre-rendered HTML; client JavaScript is limited to progressive enhancement (navigation, reveal motion, form mock behavior). Chosen by the user over runtime JS rendering. Next.js-specific items in PROJECT_SPEC.md (Next.js Image, client components) are translated to native equivalents (`<picture>`/`srcset`/`loading="lazy"`, minimal scripts).

## Users

- Parents of children considering joining a team or the football school, mostly browsing on smartphones, comparing clubs before a trial.
- Current players and their families checking fixtures, results, and news.
- Prospective sponsors and partners evaluating the club's credibility.

## Product Purpose

Official website of a Japanese youth football club (working name: サンプルFC) that runs multiple age categories (U-8 to U-18) and football schools. Success means a visitor can understand the club's philosophy and pathway, find their category or school, and reach the trial / inquiry action within seconds.

## Positioning

An academy-grade youth club presented with the editorial seriousness of a professional football club, not a hobby-team page.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Age categories and schools are data-driven; layout components never hardcode them.
- Forms have no backend: they validate and run clearly labeled prototype behavior only.
- Japanese is the primary language.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: サンプルFC (placeholder; replaceable).
- Concept from PROJECT_SPEC.md: MODERN FOOTBALL × PREMIUM ACADEMY.
- Palette is fixed by the spec: white #FFFFFF, light #F8FAFC, navy #00236a, royal blue #0054a7, muted gold #bbab54, text #1E293B, secondary text #64748B. No unrelated brand colors.

## Evidence on Hand

None. There are no real photographs, logos, results, staff, or history. All team names, scores, schedules, people, and achievements are fictional placeholders and must be labeled as such; never present invented facts as real club facts.

## Product Principles

1. The path to a trial or inquiry is always one obvious step away.
2. Structure follows data: categories, schools, matches, and news render from data, never from hand-copied markup.
3. Credibility over hype: placeholders are honest, and claims are never invented.
4. Mobile-first: a parent on a 360px phone gets the full experience.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA target (contrast, keyboard navigation, visible focus, touch targets). Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`; all content is available without animation or JavaScript.
